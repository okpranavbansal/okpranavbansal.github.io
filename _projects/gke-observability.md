A production-ready observability stack for GKE, including Helm values and Terraform configurations. Implements the "LPGT" stack (Loki, Prometheus, Grafana, Tempo) for full tracing, logging, and metrics coverage.

Key highlights:
- Standardized dashboards for GKE clusters.
- Configured log aggregation with Loki and distributed tracing with Tempo.
- Integrated with Google Cloud Workload Identity for secure access.
