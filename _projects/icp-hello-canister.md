A learning project that explores the unique architecture of the Internet Computer. Focuses on writing Motoko smart contracts (canisters) and integrating them with web frontends.

Key highlights:
- Practical experience with actors and Candid interfaces.
- Deployment to the local DFINITY execution environment.
- Understanding of the IC's query/update call cycle.
